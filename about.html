Ku Saabsan Jaamacadda Al-Azhar

Jaamacadda Al-Azhar waxay ka mid tahay xarumaha ugu horreeya ee bixiya waxbarashada sare ee tayada leh, iyadoo u diyaarisa ardayda xirfado casri ah iyo hoggaamin bulsho oo waxtar leh.

Hiigsigeenna (Vision)

Inaan noqonno jaamacadda ugu horreysa ee bixisa aqoon casri ah oo ku salaysan qiyam wanaagsan iyo hal-abuur caalami ah.

Ujeeddooyinkeenna (Mission)

Inaan u diyaarino ardayda xirfado sare oo ay ku hoggaamiyaan bulshada, suuqa shaqada, iyo horumarinta sayniska.

Qiyamka (Core Values)

Daacadnimo, xushmad, aqoon raadin joogto ah, iyo midnimo ku salaysan horumarinta mustaqbalka ardayda.
